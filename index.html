Explore art, old buildings and Italian food.

London, England

See famous sights and explore different areas.

Travel Planning

A few things to think about before you go:

1. Pick a time to travel

Check the weather and decide what season suits you.

2. Plan your transport

Look at trains, buses or flights between places.

3. Make a budget

Remember to include food, accommodation and activities.
