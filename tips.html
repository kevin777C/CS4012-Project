Small Preparations Make Better Journeys

A little planning before your trip can save you time, money and stress. On this page you will find travel tips, a packing checklist and answers to common questions.

Travel Tips

Check Travel Documents

Make sure your passport or ID is valid for your whole trip. Keep a photo of it on your phone in case you lose it.

Plan Your Spending

Set a daily budget for food, transport and activities. Bring some cash because not every place takes cards.

Know Your Transport

Europe has great trains and buses. Booking early is usually cheaper.

Preparation Before Travelling

Check the weather, download offline maps and remember that 112 is the emergency number in the EU.

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I plan a trip?

It is best to start planning two or three months before you travel. Flights and hotels are usually cheaper if you book early.

Should I carry travel insurance?

Yes. Travel insurance can help if you get sick, lose your bags or your trip is cancelled.

Where can I check official travel advice?

Check your government's travel advice website, for example the Department of Foreign Affairs in Ireland.
